What is PRISM?

Predictive Risk Intelligence & Smart Monitoring for India's national infrastructure investments under MoSPI PAIMANA.

01 • Core Engine

Deterministic Risk Intelligence

An auditable 0–100 project-health index built from observable performance signals, with zero black-box fabrication.

02 • Early Warning

Proactive Project Signals

Flags stagnation, schedule pressure, cost escalation and physical-financial divergence before deadlines expire.

03 • Analytics & AI

Forecasting & AI

Combines statistical and ML benchmarking, scenario exploration and a grounded project intelligence assistant.

04 • Decision Support

Portfolio Oversight

Helps monitoring teams identify where attention should go first across sectors, states and capital projects.
