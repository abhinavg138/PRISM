What is PRISM?

Predictive Risk Intelligence & Smart Monitoring for India's national infrastructure investments under MoSPI PAIMANA.

01 • Core Engine

Deterministic Risk Scoring

Evaluates major projects on a calibrated 0–100 scale using 6 objective mathematical indicators, with zero black-box fabrication.

02 • Problem Solved

Proactive Early Warnings

Quarterly reporting catches project delays only after deadlines expire. PRISM detects stagnation and spend disparities months in advance.

03 • Capabilities

Multi-Tier Analytics & AI

Provides 9-sector rankings, state GIS risk maps, physical-vs-spend gap analyses, and an anti-hallucination grounded AI copilot.

04 • Target Users

Cabinet & Ministry Oversight

Built for MoSPI directors, Cabinet Secretariat Project Monitoring Groups (PMG), and infrastructure agency executives.
